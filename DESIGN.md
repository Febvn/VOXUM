# DESIGN.md

> Project: **Peluit.ai — Whistle Executive Audio Transcription Console**
> Direction & Visual Identity Guide for Antigravity & AI Agents

---

## 1. Product Identity & Purpose
- **Core Domain**: Executive meeting transcription, audio intelligence, and structured decision synthesis.
- **Engine Spec**: Whistle 16.9 MB compact CTC speech decoder coupled with Gemini Flash multimodal reasoning.
- **Core Character**: High-density professional audio workstation (DAW console) meets modern dark SaaS application. Honest, fast, utilitarian, and distraction-free.

---

## 2. Palette & Visual Tokens
- **Backgrounds**:
  - Root: `#030712` (Slate-950) with subtle ambient grid / waveform accents.
  - Cards & Decks: `#0b1320` / `#0f172a` (Slate-900) with thin `#1e293b` borders.
  - Active Channel Strips: Emerald-950/60 tint with emerald border.
- **Accents**:
  - Primary Action / Live Audio: `#10b981` (Emerald-500).
  - Secondary Telemetry / Processing: `#3b82f6` (Blue-500).
  - Preset Presets / Warnings: `#f59e0b` (Amber-500).
  - Privacy / Air-gapped State: `#a855f7` (Purple-500).
- **Typography**:
  - **Headings & Technical Readouts**: `font-mono` (uppercase badges, timecodes, latency, tokens/sec, channel labels).
  - **Prose & Executive Summaries**: `font-sans` (high legibility, 1.6 line-height, neutral slate-300).

---

## 3. Strict Anti-Slop Principles
- **No Invented Metrics**: All latency numbers and model sizes reflect real measurements (16.9 MB model, measured chunk latency, real token speed). No "99.999% ROI" or "Next-Gen Quantum Synergy".
- **Functional Completeness**: Every button, slider, and tab has a functioning purpose or state (file upload, live mic recording, audio scrubbing, karaoke sync word jump, tab switching, export to Notion/Markdown/PDF/WhatsApp).
- **Tactile Ergonomics**: Mobile-first responsive layout with proper thumb-zone buttons, horizontal-scrollable tabs on narrow screens, and zero horizontal overflow.
- **Contrast & Legibility**: High contrast against dark background. No unreadable gray-on-gray text.
